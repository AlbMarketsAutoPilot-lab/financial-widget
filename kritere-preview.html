Our story

It all started on a sheet of paper.

KriTere is named after two sisters, KrisAnn & Terez.

Our first game, Don't Get Mad!, was drawn by hand by a father and his daughter, every square and every story. Game nights were so much fun that we decided to share them with every family, on every screen.

Our promise to parents

Just the game. Nothing else.

Safe to hand over

No ads, no in-app purchases and no accounts in our games. Kids just play.

Smart fun

Strategy, quiz questions and trips around the world. Learning happens on its own.

Works offline

Both apps work fully offline. Perfect for car rides, flights and holidays.

Together or solo

2 to 4 players on one device, or challenge the AI robots on your own.

Questions

Parents often ask

Anything else? Write to us on any of our social channels.

Do the games have ads or in-app purchases?

No. Don't Get Mad! and Kris Ann's Journey have no ads, no in-app purchases and need no account.

Can we play without internet?

Yes. Both games work fully offline in the Windows PC and Android apps. The web version runs right in your browser.

How many people can play?

2 to 4 players can play together on one device, or you can challenge the AI robots.

Which devices are supported?

Windows PC (Microsoft Store), Android (Google Play) and any modern web browser.

Why is it called KriTere?

KriTere is named after two sisters, KrisAnn and Terez.
