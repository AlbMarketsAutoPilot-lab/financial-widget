# KriTere pages for Blogger

Two pages for the footer links: `privacy-policy.html` and `contact.html`.

1. Replace `REPLACE_CONTACT_EMAIL` in both files with your contact email (4 times in total).
2. In Blogger go to **Pages → New page**.
3. Title: **Privacy Policy** (exactly, so the address becomes `/p/privacy-policy.html`).
4. Switch the editor to **HTML view** (the `< >` icon), paste the whole file, and click **Publish**.
5. Do the same with the title **Contact** and `contact.html` (address `/p/contact.html`).
6. Before publishing, in **Page settings → Search description**, paste:
   - Privacy Policy: `How KriTere protects families: our board games collect no personal data, have no ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no chat.`
   - Contact: `Contact KriTere, the makers of the family board games Don't Get Mad! and Kris Ann's Journey. Questions, ideas and bug reports welcome.`

   (If you don't see "Search description", turn it on first in **Settings → Meta tags → Enable search description**.)
