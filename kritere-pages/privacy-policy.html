KriTere.com ("KriTere", "we", "us") makes the family board games Don't Get Mad! and Kris Ann's Journey. This page explains, in plain words, what happens with information when you play our games and when you visit www.kritere.com.

The short version

  • Our games have no ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no chat, and they collect no personal data.
  • This website is hosted by Google Blogger and shows Google AdSense ads. Google may use cookies, as explained below.
  • We never sell information, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

1. Our games

This applies to Don't Get Mad! and Kris Ann's Journey on the web (dgm.kritere.com and kaj.kritere.com), on Windows (Microsoft Store) and on Android (Google Play).

  • No personal data. The games do not ask for your name, email, age, location, contacts or photos, and they do not collect or share personal data.
  • No accounts and no chat. There is nothing to sign up for, and players cannot talk to strangers.
  • No ads and no in-app purchases. There is nothing to buy inside the games and no advertising.
  • Your saved games stay on your device. Game progress and settings are stored only on the phone, tablet or computer you play on. Uninstalling the app or clearing your browser data removes them.
  • Works offline. The Windows and Android apps do not need an internet connection to play.

App stores. When you download a game from Google Play or the Microsoft Store, the store handles the download and your store account under its own privacy policy: Google Privacy Policy and Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Web versions. Like every website, the servers that deliver the web games may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address, browser type and time of visit) for security and to keep the service running. We do not use these logs to identify you or to build profiles.

2. This website (www.kritere.com)

Hosting. This website runs on Blogger, a service by Google. When you visit, Google processes technical information such as your IP address and browser type to deliver the pages and keep them secure. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Advertising. We use Google AdSense to show ads on this website (never inside our games). Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to show ads and to measure them. Because our website is made for families and children, we have asked Google to treat it as directed to children, which turns off personalised (interest-based) advertising. You can learn more about how Google uses cookies in advertising and how Google uses information from sites that use its services, and manage your choices in Google My Ad Center.

Cookie notice. Visitors from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for their consent before advertising cookies are used.

Trailers. Our game trailers are hosted on YouTube. We use YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode, and a video only loads when you press play. After that, the Google Privacy Policy applies to the video.

Fonts and images. Fonts are delivered by Google Fonts, and images by Google and YouTube servers. To show them, your browser connects to those servers, which receive your IP address.

Links. Our website links to Google Play, the Microsoft Store, YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, Threads and X. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

3. Children's privacy

Our games and website are made for families and children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). Our games work without any personal data. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, for example by email, please contact us and we will delete it.

Kids: please ask a parent or grown-up before writing to us or following us on social media.

4. If you contact us

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to answer you. We do not add you to mailing lists, and we do not share your message with anyone. We delete messages when we no longer need them to help you.

5. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe, the UK GDPR, or US state laws such as the CCPA, and COPPA for children in the US), you may have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. Because our games collect no personal data, we usually hold nothing about you. The only exception is an email you sent us. To make a request, contact us. For data used by Google on this website, use the Google links above.

6. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update it on this page and change the date at the top.
